# Axion customer acquisition system

## Positioning

Axion Process OS is a browser-based bioprocess engineering workspace for teams that need process design, facility operations, scheduling, TEA, LCA, evidence, versions, and decision outputs to share one model.

Do not position Axion as an unqualified universal replacement. The strongest offer is a controlled benchmark against one authorized reference process with acceptance criteria agreed before modelling.

## High-value campaigns

### Reference-model benchmark

Offer to reconstruct one customer-owned model and compare component closure, equipment duties, campaign throughput, COGS, LCA, sensitivity, traceability, and export quality. Publish results only with written approval.

### Facility-fit clinic

Invite CDMOs, MSAT, and engineering firms to bring one annual-demand and equipment-availability question. Return the bottleneck, achievable throughput, missing data, and scenario options.

### Media-cost challenge

For cultivated products, fermentation, and biopharma, compare media, feed, buffer, consumable, utility, and recovery drivers with explicit uncertainty ranges.

### Process-model readiness review

Use the ungated readiness checklist to identify missing kinetics, stream data, equipment specifications, schedule constraints, cost sources, and validation work before a design review.

## Distribution rules

- Post under a real founder, employee, or clearly labelled Axion product-team identity.
- Never invent testimonials, affiliations, academic titles, customers, benchmark results, or independent recommendations.
- Do not scrape private contact details or mass-message groups.
- Ask community moderators before promotional posting and lead with useful technical content.
- Use only customer-owned, licensed, original, or genuinely public model data.
- Name competitors accurately and keep factual claims linked to current official public documentation.

## Measurement

Review weekly:

- Non-branded search impressions and clicks by landing page.
- Comparison-to-migration click-through rate.
- Template downloads and Engineering Brief opt-ins.
- Pilot requests by role, industry, source, and campaign.
- Assessment-to-pilot and pilot-to-paid conversion.
- Time to first model, first simulation, and first complete export.
- Technical objections, failed acceptance criteria, and missing capabilities.

Use Google Search Console for search performance and a consent-compatible product analytics tool for page and product events. Do not add third-party tracking until the privacy notice, consent logic, retention, and data-processing terms are ready.
