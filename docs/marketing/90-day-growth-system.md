# Axion 90-day growth system

Updated: 2026-08-01

## Rule

Every public claim must be traceable to the deployed product, a public source, or an approved customer statement. Publish from identifiable Axion or company accounts. Do not use invented customers, fake titles, hidden affiliations, scraped private contact data, or undisclosed promotional identities.

## Primary conversion path

1. High-intent search page or technical post.
2. Free engineering resource with no download gate.
3. Axion Engineering Brief opt-in or technical pilot request.
4. Thirty-minute qualification around one customer-owned engineering decision.
5. Paid, acceptance-tested pilot.
6. Professional, Team, or Enterprise subscription.
7. Approved technical case study and referral.

## Weekly operating rhythm

| Day | Action | Asset | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Publish one engineering note | Resource page plus LinkedIn excerpt | Qualified organic visits |
| Tuesday | Invite five relevant practitioners to critique the note | Transparent one-to-one outreach | Replies and objections |
| Wednesday | Run one product walkthrough | Fifteen-minute recorded demo | Completion and pilot interest |
| Thursday | Publish one model result | Chart plus downloadable data and assumptions | Saves and resource downloads |
| Friday | Review the funnel | Search Console, leads, checkout, objections | Next week's priority |

## Twelve technical topics

1. How to define acceptance criteria for a process-simulation pilot.
2. Why utilization is not a fixed percentage in a batch facility.
3. Scheduling reactors, CIP/SIP, rooms, operators, and hold times together.
4. Media-cost sensitivity in mammalian cell culture and cultivated products.
5. Fed-batch versus perfusion: process, facility, and economic consequences.
6. What a component mass-balance residual is trying to tell you.
7. When browser CFD screening must become a validated external CFD study.
8. Oxygen-transfer and heat-removal checks before bioreactor scale-up.
9. Turning historian tags and batch records into a governed model dataset.
10. How to build a TEA/LCA export another analyst can actually audit.
11. Branches and before-after review for process variants.
12. Reconstructing an authorized reference model without proprietary files.

## Partner channels

- University bioprocess laboratories: teaching models, thesis projects, and benchmark datasets.
- Process-engineering boutiques: customer feasibility studies and reusable model libraries.
- Equipment suppliers: source-backed digital equipment objects and application notes.
- CDMOs: facility-fit pilots, capacity scenarios, and quote-ready model packages.
- Industry associations: transparent technical workshops, not product-only webinars.
- Scientific conferences: posters and reproducible model datasets linked to the resource center.

## Proof program

Each pilot should produce five possible proof assets, subject to written customer approval: a reconciled balance, a scheduling bottleneck, a before-after scenario, a sensitivity chart, and a statement of time saved. If approval is absent, publish only an anonymized method example built from original or public data and label it as such.

## Metrics

- Impressions and average position by high-intent query.
- Resource page to download rate.
- Resource page to Engineering Brief opt-in rate.
- Comparison page to pilot request rate.
- Qualified pilot requests by role, industry, source, and campaign.
- Pilot acceptance criteria passed.
- Pilot to paid conversion and expansion.
- Technical objections by frequency and product area.
